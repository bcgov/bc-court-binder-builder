PROPOSITION TO BE VERIFIED (paraphrase for drafting, not a quotation)

Courts in British Columbia have generally taken a liberal approach to amending pleadings under
Supreme Court Civil Rule 6-1(1), so that the real issues between the parties can be determined on the
merits. Leave is usually granted where the amendment is properly particularized, discloses a
reasonable claim, and any prejudice to the other side can be compensated by costs or by an
adjournment. Leave is more likely to be refused where the proposed amendment is plainly statute-barred
on the face of the record, is not properly pleaded, or would cause prejudice that cannot be remedied.
