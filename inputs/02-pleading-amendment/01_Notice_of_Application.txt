PART 2: FACTUAL BASIS

1. The Plaintiff and the Defendant entered into a written construction contract dated June 3, 2024 (the
   "Prime Contract") for the design and construction of a four-storey commercial building at 4418
   Kingsway, Burnaby, BC (the "Project").

2. The Defendant subcontracted the design, supply and installation of the building's roofing membrane,
   flashing and rooftop drainage system to Pro-Tech under a subcontract dated August 19, 2024 (the
   "Roofing Subcontract").

3. Substantial performance of the Project was certified on March 28, 2025. Since that date, the
   Plaintiff has documented recurring water ingress through the roof assembly at the north and east
   elevations, causing damage to insulation, drywall, and two tenant fit-outs.

4. On July 9, 2025, the Plaintiff gave written notice to the Defendant of the water ingress and demanded
   remediation under the Prime Contract warranty. The Defendant directed the Plaintiff to Pro-Tech, which
   is the party that installed the roof assembly.

5. An independent engineering inspection of the roof assembly was completed on September 22, 2026, by
   Coastal Building Science Ltd., which identified inadequate membrane lap detailing and undersized
   roof drains as the probable cause of the water ingress. That report is Exhibit "B" to the Tremblay
   Affidavit.

6. The Proposed Amended Claim pleads, against Pro-Tech, negligent design and installation of the roof
   assembly and breach of the duty of care owed to the Plaintiff as a foreseeable owner of the Project,
   and, against the Defendant, the additional allegations of breach of the Prime Contract warranty and
   negligent supervision of the roofing work.

7. The Plaintiff only learned of the inspection findings and the identity of the installing party's design
   responsibilities on or about September 22, 2026. The Plaintiff's pleading of those matters has not
   previously been possible on the existing record.

8. The amendments are sought at an early stage of the action. Examinations for discovery have not yet been
   scheduled, no trial date has been set, and the proposed amendments do not change the nature of the
   claim against the Defendant. Any prejudice to the Defendant or Pro-Tech can be addressed through costs
   or a short adjournment.
