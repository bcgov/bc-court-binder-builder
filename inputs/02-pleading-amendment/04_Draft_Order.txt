Response dated November 17, 2026, and the Affidavit #1 of Marcus Tremblay sworn November 2, 2026, with
the exhibits attached thereto;

IT IS ORDERED THAT:

1. The Plaintiff is granted leave to amend the Notice of Civil Claim filed March 4, 2026, in the form of
   the Amended Notice of Civil Claim attached as Exhibit "A" to the Affidavit #1 of Marcus Tremblay
   (the "Amended Claim").

   the date of this Order.

   Supreme Court Civil Rule 3-3, running from the date of service of the Amended Claim.

5. The Defendant, Cascade General Contracting Ltd., and Pro-Tech Roofing Systems Inc. shall file any
   amended Response to Civil Claim within thirty (30) days of service of the Amended Claim.
