materials filed herein, including the Notice of Application dated November 20, 2026, and Affidavit #1 
of Eleanor Vance sworn November 18, 2026;

IT IS ORDERED THAT:

1. The Respondent, Arthur Vance, shall pay to the Claimant interim child support for the children 
   Liam Vance (born May 14, 2012) and Chloe Vance (born August 22, 2015) in the amount of $2,350 per 
   month pursuant to the Federal Child Support Guidelines, commencing December 1, 2026, and payable 
   on the 1st day of each month thereafter.

2. The Respondent shall pay 65% of the children's reasonable and necessary section 7 extraordinary 
   expenses within thirty (30) days of receiving receipts or invoices from the Claimant.

3. The Respondent shall pay interim spousal support to the Claimant in the amount of $2,500 per month, 
   commencing December 1, 2026, and payable on the 1st day of each month.

4. The Respondent shall, within twenty-one (21) days of the date of this Order, deliver to the Claimant's 
   counsel full corporate disclosure for Vance Holdings Ltd., including complete financial statements, 
   T2 corporate income tax returns, and general ledger shareholder loan statements for 2024 and 2025.

5. Costs of this application shall be costs in the cause.
