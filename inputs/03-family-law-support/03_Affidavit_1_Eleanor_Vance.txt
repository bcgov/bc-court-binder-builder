AFFIDAVIT #1 OF ELEANOR VANCE

I, Eleanor Vance, of 2410 Rockland Avenue, Victoria, British Columbia, SWEAR (OR AFFIRM) THAT:

1. I am the Claimant in this proceeding. I have personal knowledge of the facts deposed to in this 
   Affidavit, except where stated to be based on information and belief.
2. The Respondent Arthur Vance and I were married on June 18, 2010. We separated on April 15, 2025.
3. We have two children together: Liam Vance (born May 14, 2012, age 14) and Chloe Vance (born 
   August 22, 2015, age 11). Both children reside with me in the former matrimonial home.
4. During our marriage, the Respondent was the principal director of Vance Holdings Ltd. He controlled 
   corporate spending, company credit cards, and determined dividend distributions.
5. In October 2026, I performed a Corporate Registry Search for Vance Holdings Ltd. (BC0984123). The 
   Notice of Articles and Annual Report confirm Arthur is the sole President and Secretary, and holds 
   100 Class A voting shares. Attached hereto and marked as Exhibit "A" to this my Affidavit is a true 
   copy of the Corporate Registry Search and Notice of Articles.
6. For the 2024 and 2025 taxation years, the Respondent reported personal line 15000 income of approximately 
   $110,000. However, internal draft balance sheets provided to our joint mortgage broker in March 2025 
   showed Vance Holdings Ltd. held unencumbered cash reserves and short-term GICs exceeding $380,000.
7. Both children have substantial medical and educational needs. Chloe was diagnosed with severe malocclusion 
   requiring two-stage orthodontic surgery costing $4,200. Liam is an elite youth soccer goalkeeper selected 
   for the Victoria Wave Metro Academy with mandatory registration and travel fees of $3,400.
8. Attached hereto and marked as Exhibit "B" to this my Affidavit is a true copy of my 2024 CRA Notice 
   of Assessment, together with invoices and receipts for the children's special and extraordinary expenses.
9. Without immediate interim child and spousal support based on the Respondent's true earning capacity, 
   I cannot maintain the matrimonial home expenses or meet the children's direct educational and sports needs.
