PART 1: ORDERS SOUGHT

1. The Respondent, Arthur Vance, shall pay to the Claimant interim child support for the two children 
   of the marriage, namely Liam Vance (born May 14, 2012) and Chloe Vance (born August 22, 2015), in 
   the amount prescribed by the Federal Child Support Guidelines for British Columbia based on an 
   imputed annual guideline income of $240,000, commencing December 1, 2026, and payable on the 1st 
   day of each and every month thereafter.

2. The Respondent shall pay his proportionate share (65%) of the children's special or extraordinary 
   expenses pursuant to section 7 of the Federal Child Support Guidelines, including competitive soccer 
   academy registration ($3,400 annually) and specialized orthodontic treatment ($4,200).

3. The Respondent shall pay interim spousal support to the Claimant pursuant to the Spousal Support 
   Advisory Guidelines (SSAG) in the monthly amount of $3,200, payable on the 1st of each month.

4. The Respondent shall, within twenty-one (21) days of the date of this Order, provide full financial 
   disclosure regarding his privately owned corporation, Vance Holdings Ltd., pursuant to section 21 
   of the Guidelines and Supreme Court Family Rule 5-1, including:
   (a) Complete corporate financial statements for the fiscal years ending 2024 and 2025;
   (b) Corporate income tax returns (T2) and Notices of Assessment for 2024 and 2025;
   (c) General ledgers detailing shareholder loan account drawdowns and management fees paid.

5. Costs of this application shall be paid by the Respondent to the Applicant.

PART 2: FACTUAL BASIS

1. The parties were married on June 18, 2010, and separated on April 15, 2025. There are two children 
   of the marriage residing primarily with the Claimant.
2. The Respondent is a 50% shareholder and managing director of Vance Holdings Ltd., a profitable 
   commercial construction consulting enterprise in Victoria, BC.
3. The Respondent claims an annual guideline income of only $110,000 based on his T4 salary draw, 
   ignoring substantial retained pre-tax corporate earnings exceeding $260,000 in Vance Holdings Ltd.
4. The children have established special expenses requiring immediate contribution, and the Claimant's 
   modest part-time employment income ($38,000) cannot sustain the mortgage and household costs.
