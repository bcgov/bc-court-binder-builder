                                   AFFIDAVIT

I, Eleanor Vance, of 4220 Commerce Way, City of Burnaby, in the Province of 
British Columbia, Chief Operating Officer, SWEAR (OR AFFIRM) THAT:

1. I am the Chief Operating Officer of the Plaintiff, Pacific Rim Holdings Ltd. 
   ("Pacific Rim"), and as such I have personal knowledge of the matters hereinafter 
   deposed to, except where stated to be on information and belief, and where so 
   stated I verily believe the same to be true.

2. On October 14, 2024, I negotiated and executed on behalf of Pacific Rim a Master 
   Commercial Warehousing & Logistics Agreement with the Defendant, North Shore 
   Logistics Inc. ("North Shore"). Attached hereto and marked as Exhibit "A" to this 
   my Affidavit is a true copy of the said Agreement.

3. Pursuant to Section 14.2 of Exhibit "A", Pacific Rim was billed tiered warehousing 
   rates based on the representation that North Shore continuously maintained a 
   minimum dedicated threshold of 120,000 cubic feet of climate-controlled space.

4. Between February 2025 and May 2026, Pacific Rim received twenty-eight (28) written 
   notices from its retail grocery distributors stating that inbound refrigerated 
   consignments were being refused or delayed at North Shore's Richmond terminal 
   due to claimed facility overload.

5. Despite these alleged overloads, North Shore invoiced Pacific Rim for maximum 
   premium capacity utilization rates during every single billing cycle throughout 
   that period, totalling $1,124,500.00.

6. On August 18, 2026, our legal counsel received North Shore's initial List of 
   Documents. When I reviewed the production, I observed that North Shore had completely 
   omitted all underlying daily inbound/outbound yard logs and had applied complete 
   black-box redactions across every single quarterly operational audit.

7. Without access to unredacted audit figures and inventory volume counts, Pacific Rim 
   cannot cross-examine the Defendant's logistics managers or compute its damages 
   arising from the billing overcharges.

8. On September 22, 2026, our solicitors sent a formal demand letter requesting the 
   production of these specific documents within 14 days. Attached hereto and marked 
   as Exhibit "B" to this my Affidavit is a true copy of the demand letter. 

9. To date, the Defendant has refused to produce the documents demanded.
