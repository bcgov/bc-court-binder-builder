                                APPLICATION RESPONSE

Application response of: North Shore Logistics Inc. (Defendant)

THIS IS A RESPONSE to the Notice of Application of the Plaintiff filed October 15, 2026.


Part 1: ORDERS CONSENTED TO

None.


Part 2: ORDERS OPPOSED

The defendant opposes all relief sought in paragraphs 1, 2, and 3 of Part 1 of 
the Notice of Application.


Part 3: ORDERS PROPOSED

1. The application be dismissed with costs to the defendant in any event of the cause.

2. In the alternative, if production of the Quarterly Operations and Logistics Audit 
   Reports is ordered, production be limited strictly to Section 4 ("Richmond Facility 
   Throughput") and be subject to a two-tier confidentiality ring restricting inspection 
   exclusively to independent legal counsel and independent accounting experts retained 
   herein.


Part 4: FACTUAL BASIS

1. The defendant's initial List of Documents delivered August 18, 2026, fully complies 
   with Rule 7-1(1) by listing all documents that could prove or disprove a material 
   fact in issue.

2. The third-party pallet logs demanded by the plaintiff contain proprietary pricing 
   schedules and inventory ratios belonging to non-party commercial clients who utilize 
   the Richmond terminal under strict non-disclosure covenants.

3. The unredacted portions of the Quarterly Audit Reports contain proprietary corporate 
   margin formulas completely irrelevant to the fee overcharge claim pleaded by the 
   plaintiff.

4. Compelling blanket, unredacted production without a protective confidentiality order 
   would inflict serious commercial prejudice upon the defendant in an intensely 
   competitive market.


Part 5: LEGAL BASIS

1. Supreme Court Civil Rules, Rule 7-1(11), 7-1(18), and Rule 1-3 (Proportionality).
2. The court retains inherent jurisdiction and discretion under Rule 7-1(18) to deny 
   production or impose terms restricting access to commercially sensitive records 
   not directly relevant to the core issues in the pleadings.


Part 6: MATERIAL TO BE RELIED ON

1. Affidavit #1 of Derek Chen, filed October 23, 2026.
2. Pleadings filed herein.

The respondent estimates that the application will take 30 minutes.
