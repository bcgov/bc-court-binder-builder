ON THE APPLICATION of the Plaintiff, Pacific Rim Holdings Ltd., coming on for hearing 
at the Courthouse at 800 Smithe Street, Vancouver, British Columbia (via Virtual Chambers 
video conference) on this day, and on hearing Marcus A. Sterling, counsel for the 
Plaintiff, and Kendra T. MacIntyre, counsel for the Defendant; AND ON READING 
Affidavit #1 of Eleanor Vance sworn October 14, 2026, and the pleadings filed herein:

IT IS ORDERED THAT:

1. Within fourteen (14) days of the date of this Order, the Defendant, North Shore 
   Logistics Inc., prepare, file, and serve upon the Plaintiff an amended List of 
   Documents in Form 22 listing all daily inventory volume counts, yard tracking 
   spreadsheets, and third-party freight throughput logs for the period between 
   January 1, 2025, and June 30, 2026.

2. Within fourteen (14) days of the date of this Order, the Defendant produce for 
   inspection and copying unredacted versions of its Internal Quarterly Operations 
   and Logistics Audit Reports for Q1 2025, Q2 2025, Q3 2025, Q4 2025, Q1 2026, 
   and Q2 2026.

3. The costs of this application be awarded to the Plaintiff in any event of the cause 
   assessed at Scale B.
