When you file

  • In the CSO E-file menu choose Application Records, not Submit Package.
  • Submit the binder as one PDF. Give it sequence number 1; a book of authorities is 2. Reusing a number overwrites the earlier upload, and a corrected record must reuse the same number.
  • Deadline: 4:00 p.m. one full business day before the hearing. Late records are not placed on the hearing list.
  • Fee: $7.00 transaction fee plus any statutory fees, charged to your registered card. Keep the confirmation email.
