What happens when you run it

  1. 1Generate filings. A notice of application, application response, affidavit with exhibits, draft order and a case authority.
  2. 2Build the binder. Cover, index with links, page numbers at top centre, and bookmarks.
  3. 3Check 20 court gates. You get a pass or fail result for each one.
