## Executive Summary

This workflow documents standard law firm procedure for assembling a British Columbia Supreme Court chambers application record under Practice Direction 72 (PD-72) and Court Services Online (CSO) rules.

## The 9-Step Standard Procedure

In standard manual Adobe Acrobat practice, a senior paralegal performs a **9-step process taking 45–90 minutes per filing**:
1. Manual file naming and ordering
2. Acrobat content compilation
3. Outline bookmarking and exhibit nesting
4. Header & Footer folio stamping (Top-Center 14–16 pt Bold Arial)
5. Calculating folios, manually typing them into Word TOC, and exporting to PDF
6. Inserting Cover & Index at front of document
7. Configuring Page Labels (None for preliminaries, 1..N for tabs)
8. Drawing link rectangles line-by-line across Index entries
9. File size auditing against CSO threshold (< 50 MB)

Our automated  engine performs this complete synthesis deterministically in **under 200 milliseconds**.
